Online store · Checkout · Shipping

PHP/Bootstrap E-commerce Optimization — Checkout, Payments, and Postal Code Shipping Quotes

I optimized an online store built with PHP and Bootstrap, improving the cart, checkout, user registration, guest purchases, Mercado Pago and bank transfer payments, minimum-order-weight validations, free shipping by weight, and responsive layout.

I implemented a postal code-based shipping calculator using a rate table. It takes order weight, delivery method, and destination category into account, with options for home delivery, branch pickup, and manual delivery arrangements with the seller.

I also tested the complete purchase flow, including shipping calculations, passing shipping costs to payment, validating Mercado Pago amounts, and confirmation emails for the customer and store administrator.
