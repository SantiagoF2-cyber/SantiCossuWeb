About Me

Hi! I'm Santiago Cossu, a web developer. I enjoy turning ideas into websites and digital solutions that not only look great but are also useful, fast, and easy to use.

I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and Node.js, as well as platforms like GoHighLevel and HubSpot. I have experience building custom websites and landing pages, configuring CRM systems, handling integrations, and improving existing projects.

I'm detail-oriented, responsible, and committed to every project I take on. I enjoy listening to clients, understanding their needs, and maintaining clear, friendly communication throughout the entire process.

Whether it's building something from scratch, improving an existing website, or solving a technical issue, my goal is always the same: to find a solution that truly works and leaves the client happy with the result.
